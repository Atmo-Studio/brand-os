# Purpose, Vision & Mission

This is the foundation of the brand. Purpose is why. Vision is where. Mission is what. A healthy brand can trace any major decision back through all three without contradiction.

## Brand purpose

Define why the brand exists, beyond making money. Why does this need to exist?

> [Brand] exists to ______.

## Vision

A visualization of the mission that sets an internal guidepost for the brand's aspirations. The worldview and philosophy that shapes how this brand builds and shows up.

> To [aspirational future state].

## Mission

An ambitious goal to achieve the purpose. What this brand actually does, for whom, toward what outcome. What the brand is committing to today.

> To [verb] [audience] [toward what outcome].
