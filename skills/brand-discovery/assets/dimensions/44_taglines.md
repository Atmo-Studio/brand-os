# Taglines

Taglines are the sharpest compression of the brand's positioning and pillars into language that travels. Unlike pillars — which are durable and structural — taglines cycle. They're built for campaigns, contexts, and moments.

> **Tagline principles**
> - A tagline is not a mission statement. It's a punch, not an explanation.
> - Strong taglines should feel like something the audience might say themselves.
> - If a tagline could belong to any brand in the category, it's not sharp enough.
> - Evergreen phrases belong in Messaging Pillars. This dimension is for campaign-level copy that earns its rotation.
