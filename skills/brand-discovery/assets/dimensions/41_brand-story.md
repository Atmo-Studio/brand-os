# Brand Story

The best brand communication tells the customer's story, not the brand's. The brand acts as a guide. This structure gives every brand communication just enough of a shared narrative skeleton.

## The 7-Part Structure

| Element | This brand's version |
|---|---|
| The character | [Who the customer is and what they want] |
| The problem | [External / internal / philosophical] |
| The guide (us) | [Our empathy + our authority] |
| The plan | [The clear 3-step path we offer] |
| Call to action | [Direct CTA + transitional CTA] |
| Success | [What their life looks like after] |
| Failure | [What's at stake if they don't act] |

## How to use this page

Before writing anything of substance, identify: (1) Who is the character, (2) What problem are we acknowledging, (3) How does our plan resolve it? If those three aren't clear, the content isn't ready to write.
